Hi!

I'm a computer scientist with a background in theoretical CS and a particular interest in graphs and complexity. As a researcher at the [Chair for Logic and Theory of Discrete Systems](https://www.lics.rwth-aachen.de) at RWTH Aachen University, I worked on three projects at this intersection:
- analysing privacy vulnerabilities in vector embeddings of social networks
- studying the complexity of constructing graphs with prescribed subgraph counts ([paper](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.STACS.2024.19))
- and investigating the complexity of learning logical formulas on graph structures in a formal supervised learning framework ([paper](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.CSL.2025.8))

I've also given [talks](/talks/) on this work and have experience in academic [teaching](/teaching/).

I'm currently looking for new challenges outside of academia.
